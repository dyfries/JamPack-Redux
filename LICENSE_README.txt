What This Is
-------------
The Jam Pack is a collection of ready-to-use game development assets designed for:
- Game jams
- Student projects
- Educational use in classrooms (High School & College)

It is free to use in your games or lessons, but comes with a few simple rules.

How You Can Use It
-------------------
- Use in games, projects, or lessons
- Modify, combine, or adapt the assets
- Include in commercial or non-commercial work

What You Cannot Do
------------------
- Sell, sublicense, or redistribute the assets on their own
  (They must be part of a larger work, e.g., a game, lesson, or project)
- Remove attribution

How to Credit
-------------
- In your README or project notes:
  "These assets are licensed under the Jam Pack Open License (JPOL) – CC BY 4.0 with No Standalone Resale. Full license in LICENSE.txt."

- In-game / on-screen credit (short version):
  "Some assets © [Your Name], used under Jam Pack Open License (JPOL). Do not sell separately."

Read it carefully if you plan to redistribute or use commercially.

Why This License Works
----------------------
- Lets students and developers use the assets freely
- Supports commercial games and lessons
- Prevents standalone resale, keeping the pack from being asset-flipped or resold
